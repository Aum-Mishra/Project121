ApexPetro Refining Complex - Coastal Refinery, Unit 2
Maintenance Notes - Asset P-102 (Feed Transfer Pump)
Location: Pump Bay 2 / Unit 2
Department: Maintenance / Inspection & Integrity

Date: 07 March 2026

Note:

During routine condition monitoring on 07 March 2026, the driven-end (DE) vibration
reading on P-102 was recorded at 5.6 mm/s. This exceeds the applicable action
threshold of 4.5 mm/s for this pump.

Given the exceedance, a repeat measurement was taken on the same shift to rule out
a spurious reading. The repeat measurement remained above the 4.5 mm/s limit,
confirming a genuine high-vibration condition rather than a sensor or measurement
error.

Following confirmation of the sustained high reading, a maintenance inspection was
requested through the work-order system. The Maintenance team, supported by
Inspection & Integrity, carried out a physical inspection of the pump-motor set.

Inspection finding: the inspection identified a coupling alignment issue between the
P-102 pump shaft and the driving motor. Misalignment of this kind is a common root
cause of elevated DE-side vibration on centrifugal pump trains.

Corrective action: the coupling alignment was corrected using standard shaft
alignment procedures (laser alignment check against OEM tolerances).

Post-maintenance verification: following the alignment correction, DE vibration was
re-measured and found to be 3.9 mm/s, which is within the 4.5 mm/s operating limit.

Disposition: P-102 was returned to service. A monitoring period was put in place to
confirm the vibration trend remains stable following the coupling alignment
correction, with follow-up condition-monitoring readings scheduled per the standard
inspection cadence.

Reference operating limits for P-102:
- Discharge pressure: 18-22 bar
- DE vibration: <= 4.5 mm/s
- Bearing temperature: <= 85 degC
- Flow rate: 450-500 m3/h

Asset reference data:
- Motor: 315 kW
- Speed: 1485 RPM
- Design flow: 475 m3/h

End of note.
