# MRPL HR Policy Handbook — Demo

## Leave
Demo annual entitlement: 24 days. Sick leave: 12 days. Requests require the approved workflow and manager authorization.

## Recruitment
Approved requisition → screening → interview/assessment → documented selection approval → offer → onboarding.

## Performance
Reviews consider role objectives, measurable outcomes, safety/compliance behavior, collaboration and development needs.

## Training
Mandatory safety, security and compliance training should remain current.

## Employee relations
Grievances and disciplinary matters are confidential and handled by authorized HR personnel.

## Data protection
Employee information is confidential and access-controlled. AI retrieval must apply authorization before content reaches a model.
